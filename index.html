Calculate your body mass index from weight and height.

Your BMI0
Category0

How to use the bmi calculator

Enter your weight in kilograms and height in centimetres. BMI is weight divided by height squared, and the categories follow the standard WHO ranges for adults.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. It is a rough screening number and does not measure body fat or muscle. Talk to a doctor for health advice.

About SmartTools

SmartTools is a collection of simple, free tools for everyday tasks: counting words, working out an age or a loan instalment, converting units, making QR codes and strong passwords.

Every tool runs in your browser. We do not ask you to sign up, and the text and numbers you enter are not stored on our servers.
